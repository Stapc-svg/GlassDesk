GlassDesk brings frosted-glass widgets to your Android home screen, matching the GlassDesk desktop organizer for Windows and Linux.

Widgets:
• Fences – group your favourite apps in a glass box
• Clock – big, clean time and date
• Weather – current conditions for your city
• Calendar
• Notes and To-do list
• Countdown to an event
• System info
• Daily quote

Features:
• Many glass themes, light and dark
• Resizable widgets that adapt to their size
• No ads, no tracking, no account
• Free and open source (MIT)
